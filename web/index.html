The odds for each mark

Each bar is the decision-maker's probability for one option; the picked option is marked. The pick sharpens these odds (T = 0.5) and draws with the seed.

Probability map settings

Style

Impasto: thick oil paint; each stroke's colour is drawn from its cell's odds, and uncertain cells get thicker paint.

The rules fill every cell at once.

Pixel colour

Most likely makes clean blocks; sampling shows more of each cell's colour probabilities.

Brighter areas have more concentrated colour odds; darker areas are less certain.

Model map settings

The model answers cells in sequence, then the odds are smoothly expanded to your full map. A smaller grid is faster; a larger grid gives it more decisions to make.

Honest note: a small model deciding every cell is the hardest job here; expect rough results. In earlier tests small models drifted towards dull, mid-value palettes, and the rules usually look better.

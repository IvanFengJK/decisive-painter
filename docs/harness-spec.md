## Phases and budget

50 marks. backdrop 1-8: lay in the sky, wall or overall ground colour; ground 9-16: the land, water, floor or table; distance 17-24: the horizon, far shapes and middle distance; subject 25-40: the main subject at the focal place; accents 41-50: details, highlights, dark accents and texture.

## Upfront questions (one pass, both option orders averaged, T = 0.5)

- **mood**: "Which mood should the picture have?" options: calm (quiet and peaceful); stormy (dark, wild weather); warm (sunny, cosy heat); eerie (strange, uneasy, ghostly); joyful (bright and happy); austere (plain, sparse, severe)
- **harmony**: "Which colour harmony should the palette use?" options: analogous (neighbouring hues); complementary (a hue and its opposite); split-complementary (a hue and the two hues beside its opposite); triadic (three evenly spaced hues); monochrome (one hue in light and dark)
- **hue**: "Which hue family should dominate the picture?" options: red; orange; yellow; green; teal; blue; violet; pink
- **key**: "How light or dark should the picture be overall?" options: light (mostly pale values); mid (middle values); dark (mostly deep values)
- **saturation**: "How strong should the colours be?" options: muted (soft, greyish colour); moderate (natural colour); vivid (intense, pure colour)
- **style**: "Which painting style should the picture have?" options: loose open-air sketch (quick and airy, edges left unfinished); quiet tonal study (few colours, soft changes of light and dark); bold poster (flat strong shapes and clear silhouettes); dreamy wet-on-wet (soft blooms and blurred edges); nervous ink drawing (many fine scratchy lines); naive folk picture (simple symbolic shapes, bright and direct); moody nocturne (dark, a few lights, heavy atmosphere); hot expressive colour (saturated, energetic, unnatural colour); flat woodblock (flat bands of colour with outlines, decorative); dotted light (built from many small touches of colour); childlike crayon (wobbly playful lines); atmospheric haze (faint layered washes and deep distance)
- **composition**: "Which composition fits the request best?" options: open horizon (a level horizon across the middle, sky above and land or sea below, subject near the centre); tall centre (one tall subject standing in the middle, flanked by space on both sides); big sky (a low horizon with a large sky; a high light or moon; small land at the bottom); deep ground (a high horizon; most of the picture is ground or water receding into the distance); side weight (the main mass on the left, an answering smaller shape on the right, open space between); still life (a close view of an object on a table, a plain wall behind); mirror water (a scene above a still water surface that repeats it below); diagonal sweep (a strong diagonal from lower left to upper right, with movement along it)
- **stroke**: "What character should the marks have?" options: calm and even (steady, regular marks); energetic and loose (long fast marks that wander); precise and controlled (tight, careful, evenly spaced marks); rough and broken (dry, broken, scratchy marks); soft and blended (marks that melt into each other)

- Harmony → hue offsets of (secondary, accent): {"analogous": [30.0, -35.0], "complementary": [180.0, 165.0], "split-complementary": [150.0, 210.0], "triadic": [120.0, 240.0], "monochrome": [0.0, 0.0]}
- Roles: dominant (L0, C0, H); secondary (L0 − 0.14 if L0 > 0.55 else L0 + 0.16, 0.8·C0, H + offset); accent (0.52 if L0 ≥ 0.6 else 0.72, min(0.22, max(1.6·C0, 0.08·chroma factor)), H + offset); neutral (0.82 / 0.70 / 0.55 by key, 0.022, H); shadow (0.18 if dark key else 0.24, min(0.06, C0), H pulled 30° cool); highlight (0.86, 0.06 if vivid else 0.045, H pulled 40° warm). L clamped to [0.18, 0.92]; chroma reduced in 0.004 steps until the colour is inside sRGB. Paper: OKLCH (0.965, 0.012, 85.0) = #f7f3eb.
- Words: value = deep (L < 0.30), dark (< 0.45), mid (< 0.70), light (< 0.86), very light; temperature = neutral if C < 0.03, else warm if hue < 115° or ≥ 330°, else cool; hue word = nearest family (charcoal / grey / white when neutral; sand when warm, C < 0.07 and L > 0.70).
- Mapping rule for old palettes (controls, re-renders): the engine settings nearest the old five colours by a symmetric OKLab chamfer distance; an old colour → the nearest role colour (research table, not included).

## Per-mark questions

- Asked of the model (L2): gesture, place, colour; code sets size by phase {"backdrop": "large", "ground": "large", "distance": "medium", "subject": "medium", "accents": "small"}, weight by phase {"backdrop": "light", "ground": "medium", "distance": "medium", "subject": "heavy", "accents": "heavy"}, fill 'solid fill', medium by style {"loose open-air sketch": "watercolour", "quiet tonal study": "watercolour", "dreamy wet-on-wet": "watercolour", "atmospheric haze": "watercolour", "nervous ink drawing": "ink pen", "bold poster": "oil pastel", "naive folk picture": "oil pastel", "moody nocturne": "oil pastel", "hot expressive colour": "oil pastel", "flat woodblock": "oil pastel", "dotted light": "oil pastel", "childlike crayon": "oil pastel"}; 50 marks always.
- Gesture menus by phase (L1): backdrop: horizontal band, round disc, broad wash, slanted strokes, dotted speckle, stacked arcs; ground: horizontal band, low mound, broad wash, vertical hatching, horizontal dashes, slanted strokes, dotted speckle, stacked arcs; distance: horizontal band, upright column, low mound, pointed triangle, block, horizontal dashes, dotted speckle, stacked arcs; subject: upright column, round disc, tall oval, wide oval, low mound, pointed triangle, block, wedge, outline loop; accents: round disc, vertical hatching, horizontal dashes, slanted strokes, cross-hatching, dotted speckle, flowing ribbon, outline loop.
- Place menus (L1): backdrop / ground / distance: the composition's suggested named places for the phase; subject: those + the grid cells ≥ 50 % inside them; accents: those + the subject's cells.
- Gesture descriptions: horizontal band: a wide flat strip across the place; upright column: a tall narrow block; round disc: a filled circle; tall oval: an upright ellipse; wide oval: a flat ellipse; low mound: a rounded hump rising from the bottom of the place; pointed triangle: a triangle pointing up; block: a rectangle; wedge: a slanted triangular slice; broad wash: a soft covering of the whole place; vertical hatching: several upright strokes; horizontal dashes: several level strokes; slanted strokes: several diagonal strokes; cross-hatching: two crossing sets of lines; dotted speckle: many small dots; flowing ribbon: one long wavy stroke; stacked arcs: a few curved arcs, like waves or hills; zigzag: a sharp back-and-forth line; outline loop: a closed outline around the place; tangled scribble: a loose knot of lines.
- Question wording (Round 12): gesture "Which mark should the next gesture make?"; place "Where should the mark go: a named place of the composition, or one cell of the 5x5 grid?"; colour "Which palette role should the mark use?" (A1).
- Yes/no scoring (A2; not in the chosen set): "Is {} a good next mark?" / "Is {} a good place for the next mark?" per menu candidate, score = p(yes), normalised over the menu.
- Final distribution: q(o) ∝ p_model(o)^(1/T) · prior_phase(o)^b over the menu, T = 0.5, b = 1.0; prior_phase = Round 14's L3 table (**informed by a closed reference model's cached decisions; not included**) mixed 1 % with uniform. The site does not use this prior: its phase weights come from Kev-4B (`docs/LICENSES.md`). Pick: numpy `default_rng(seed_of(painting id, step, question, mode)).choice`.

## State text (L4 short state)

Line 1: request, style (description), stroke, palette sentence, composition (description). Line 2: mark n of 50, phase (purpose) and the suggested places. Then the 5×5 read-back, one line per row, equal neighbouring cells merged ('2-4 mostly …'), and the last 4 marks (gesture at place, colour).
Read-back rule (Round 12): every 3rd pixel → nearest of paper / the colours / their half-way tints to the paper in CIELAB; per cell coverage < 5 % 'empty paper', < 35 % 'a little X on paper', < 75 % 'X[, some Y], paper showing', else 'mostly X[, some Y]' (Y when ≥ 20 % of the paint); with A1 each painted cell ends with its main colour's words in parentheses.

An example state written by the site's own RuleDirector is in `docs/DIRECTOR.md` (the research example, a recorded model run, is not included).

## Encoding for Kev (kev.model.encode)

`<|fim_prefix|>` state, then per question `<|fim_middle|>` instruction, per option `<|box_start|>` option text (`name: description`) `<|box_end|>`, then `<|fim_suffix|>`. Branches attend to the state and themselves only; probabilities come from the pointer head at the `<|box_end|>` and `<|fim_suffix|>` positions. Yes/no questions have the two options `no`, `yes`. Caller text has `<|name|>` rewritten to `<¦name¦>` before tokenising.

## Renderer

`harness/render.py` (Pillow + numpy; frozen since Round 12). Every random choice is seeded with sha256(painting id, step) (paper: sha256(painting id, 'paper')). The research traces (not included) carry the PNG SHA-256 for a byte-for-byte port check. The site's port (`web/render.js`) uses a portable RNG instead; its Python twin is `harness/render_portable.py`.
